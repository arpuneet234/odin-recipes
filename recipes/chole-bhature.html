Chole Bhature

Description

North India's most loved breakfast — spicy chole served with hot fluffy bhature!

Ingredients

  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Onion, tomato & spices
  • Bhature dough

Steps

  1. Cook chole with onion, tomato & spices.
  2. Roll bhature and deep fry till fluffy.
  3. Serve hot with onions & pickle!
