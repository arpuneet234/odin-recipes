Pizza

Description

A delicious cheesy pizza with crispy crust and your favorite toppings!

Ingredients

  • Pizza base
  • Cheese
  • Vegetables / Toppings
  • Pizza Sauce

Steps

  1. Spread pizza sauce over the pizza base.
  2. Add cheese and vegetables on top.
  3. Bake for 10–15 minutes.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
